# Existing-software overlap check

Checked 6 November 2026 in response to Paul's question about whether existing software captures a large portion of Ivo's proposed functionality. This is a review of current primary product documentation, not a hands-on evaluation, exhaustive market search or proof that a feature is absent. No apps were installed or accounts connected.

Substantial overlap exists. Ivo should not be justified by claiming that linked visual documents, spatial notes, vector editing or mobile design tools are individually new. The proposed combination and workflow fit must be assessed together.

## Assessment

The gap suggested by this review is the combination of precise multi-page composition and editable vector masks, a linked knowledge system/graph, scans and region links, polished fashion-oriented typography/color/spacing, Mac/iPhone/iPad editing, existing-cloud-provider choice and faithful PDF output. This is a product-fit assessment, not a claim of universal market uniqueness or a measured feature-completion percentage.

Curio and Affinity are the first useful benchmarks before a broader Ivo rewrite. Use one representative fashion/research document: lay out differently sized pages, align and reshape a polygon, crop content inside it, preserve typography, link a note/scan region, navigate relationships, move between devices and export a shareable PDF. Record which parts succeed, fail or require a second app. Test provider saving/sync separately. Do not reject a potentially adequate existing workflow merely to continue custom development.

Personal/friends use does not require commercial novelty. Continuing Ivo can be justified by a better integrated workflow and the user's specific interaction/visual requirements, while reusing mature libraries where appropriate. It should not be based on an assumption that no existing software covers most of the individual mechanics.
